# Seed taxonomy vs. measured structures

314 host systems have both a seed hypothesis and at least one ICSD-backed space group.

- prototype space group **present** among those measured: **259/314** (82%)
- prototype space group is the **best-attested** one: **196/314** (62%)

The reference says which phases exist in a chemistry, not which phase a paper made, so a disagreement is a prompt to review rather than proof of an error. The Al-O-Zn row below is the clearest example.
